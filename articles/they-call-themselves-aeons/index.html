The word

They call themselves Aeons

The name arrived with a secret NFT mint in November 2023. Community pages say it means eternity. The dictionary, and the paper trail, are a little more complicated.

Flip the Stock24 November 2026

Share on X

People who hold SPX6900, or who want to be counted among them, call themselves Aeons. The project site uses the word the same way. So do the Sunday Spaces, the meetups, and the greetings at the end of posts. It is the crowd's name for itself. How it got there is less settled than the name is now.

In ordinary English, an aeon is a very long age. The spelling is the Commonwealth one; Americans usually write eon. Dictionaries trace it through Latin aeon to the Greek αἰών (aiōn), a word that started as a lifetime and came to mean an age, an era, or eternity. A community about page at aeons6900.com puts it more simply, and a little short of that path: Aeon, it says, is "the name people give to those holding SPX token, it means eternity in ancient latin." Latin did borrow the word, and Late Latin used it for age and eternity. The root is Greek. The same dictionaries also record a Gnostic sense, a spirit being emanating from the Godhead. No official post or project page cited here uses that meaning as the source of this name.

What the public record does show is a date. On 16 November 2023, the official @spx6900 account presented "our first secret project reveal: Project Aeon," a collection of 3,333 pieces: 1,111 free mints, 1,111 discount mints, and 1,111 public, the first two limited to a snapshot. A community timeline on that same about page dates the launch to 15 November, which lines up with the same evening in North America. A few hours later the official account said the collection had minted out. The lore written around those tokens is science fiction, not scripture: a fictional "quantum glitch" at SPX6900 Labs that sparked 3,333 beings "neither of this world nor wholly apart from it," as the collection's story tells it. The official links page calls them "3,333 divine beings." The commandments page later describes aeons as "our custom on-chain identities," the line of mascots after Marie Rose.

Who chose the word is not a matter of a single signed claim. A later official post, in July 2026, called the collection "a community-driven secret project under the creative direction of @PankoPunk, delivered in November 2023 to provide a visual identity to SPX6900." On the night it minted out, the same official account thanked @baka_console and asked people to reply "Thank You Denko!" Those are credits for the work. They are not, on their own, a statement of who first said the holders should be called Aeons. A search of public posts from the token's August 2023 launch until the mint did not turn up the word being used as a name for the crowd. That does not prove nobody said it in a chat or a Space. It does mean the paper trail on X starts with the collection.

The jump from token to people was quick. By late December 2023 the official account was addressing the crowd in the singular, as if the word were already a greeting: "buckle up, aeon." "ily aeon" and "ily aeons," a shorthand for "I love you," show up in community posts within days of the mint. A later Reddit glossary treats Aeon as both a devoted member and a holder of one of the 3,333 tokens, and lists "Ilyaeon" as the same affection written as one word. The two senses were not kept apart. The picture became a profile. The profile became a person.

The rest of the vocabulary hung off that name. Cognisphere, on the community about page, is "the shared consciousness where Aeons Plug themselves to form One Unique Entity." The official about page puts it in the project's own voice: "The risk averse are rejected by Lumina at Cognisphere's Gate, we march with full conviction." By early 2024 the official account was telling people to enter the cognisphere, and the Project Aeon account used the same line to open a Discord. A poetic style of self-description, "Aeon of the SPX6900 Cognisphere," is still in circulation. Event names followed the root: Mondaeon for a regular Monday call, CarolinAeon for a gathering in South Carolina. Persist forever, a slogan the official commandments page traces to a remark by RaoulGMI, is tied on the community about page to the idea that Aeons already means eternity. Those are two accounts of the same phrase. They can sit beside each other.

Flip the stock market is the mission the name is supposed to outlast. The word does two jobs at once, which is probably why it stuck. It is a character you can put on, and a claim that you mean to stay for a long time. Whether that is a lifetime, an age, or eternity depends on which page you are reading. This is a retelling for a community site. It is not an offer, and nothing here is a reason to buy a token or a picture of one.
