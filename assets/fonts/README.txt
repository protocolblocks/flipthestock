These Latin-subset woff2 files are the fonts used on flipthestock.com,
taken from the families and weights previously loaded from Google Fonts:

  Frank Ruhl Libre 500
  Newsreader 500 and 600 (optical size 6–72)
  Public Sans 400, 500, 600 and 700

Each family is licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.
The copyright notices and the full licence text are in OFL.txt in this folder.
